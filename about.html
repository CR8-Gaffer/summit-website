The maintenance partner, not the callout.

Summit is an Adelaide company built around one idea: the condition of the assets a site is accountable for should not live in someone’s memory. It should be measured, documented and owned.

Who we are

We maintain the assets a site is accountable for — and the evidence that proves it.

Most kitchens buy a service. Summit provides the record — the difference between a job that was done and a document that proves it, held current for the audit, the insurer and the Form 3.

The work divides in two, and the program says which is which. Compliance work — kitchen exhaust systems — runs to an interval set by measurement and closes with a certificate. Condition work — the discharge zone, bin rooms, docks and entries — runs to a cycle set by use and closes with the found and finished condition on record. Both sit on the same calendar.

Summit is owner-operated. The person who surveys your kitchen is the person who answers the phone.

03Standards and coverage

Held to the same standard we hold your site.

01
AS 1851-2012

The only edition cited on any Summit document. Section 13 — fire and smoke control features of mechanical services.

02
AIRAH Best Practice Guide (2022)

Commercial Kitchen Exhaust Management: no single post-clean test above 50 μm, system mean below 200 μm between services, local deposits of 2,000 μm or more cleaned urgently — attributed to the guide, never to the standard’s text.

03
