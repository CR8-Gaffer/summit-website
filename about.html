The maintenance partner, not the callout.

Summit is an Adelaide company built around one idea — the condition of the assets a site is accountable for shouldn’t live in someone’s memory. It should be measured, documented and owned.

Who we are

We maintain the assets a site is accountable for — and the evidence that proves it.

Built for
  • Hotel groups
  • Aged care
  • Shopping centres and food courts
  • Venue operators — licensed venues and clubs
  • Facilities and property managers
  • Single venues and small groups, welcome

Most kitchens buy a service. Summit provides the record — the difference between a job that was done and a document that proves it, held current for the audit, the insurer and the Form 3.

The work divides in two, and Summit never confuses them. Compliance work — kitchen exhaust systems — runs to an interval set by measurement, and closes with a certificate. Condition work — the discharge zone, bin rooms, loading docks, carpark and entries — runs to a cycle set by how hard the area is used, and closes with the found and finished condition on record. One is a legal obligation and one is not; the program says which is which, and both sit on the same calendar.

For a group or a facilities team, that means one schedule, one invoice and one set of records across every site — the compliance admin lifted off your desk, and nothing left to memory. On a multi-tenant site — every tenancy kitchen, the common exhaust, and the surfaces in between — that is the site planner.
