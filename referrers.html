01Why it matters to your clients

Three trades. One document.

Insurance brokers

Claims-ready evidence, on file before it is needed

After a fire, the claims assessor asks six things: what was done, when, to what standard, measured how, by whom, and what was known to be outstanding. A Summit certificate answers all six from the document alone — a cleaner conversation at renewal, and one less unknown in the risk being placed.

Fire-suppression technicians

Work flowing to your trade, in writing

You see the exhaust system at every suppression service, and its condition is your working environment. Where a survey records a suppression-side deficiency, it is documented for the venue and referred back to their suppression technician in writing — to your trade, not around it.

Installers & food-safety consultants

A baseline your recommendation can stand on

A new canopy or a food-safety audit is the moment a venue’s exhaust file gets opened. A measured baseline gives your recommendation something to stand on, and the venue a record that starts on day one.

02What a referral looks like

Simple and professional. The whole ask is an introduction.

You mention Summit where it genuinely fits. Summit delivers a free site survey to qualifying venues, and the venue keeps the written report whether or not they engage further. No scripts, no targets, no obligation in either direction — and you see the standard of the documentation before your name is ever near it.

The report never names, grades or characterises any current provider: it measures the system and writes down what it finds.
