High-access condition work,
without the scaffold.

Façade and high-access work delivered by drone — no scaffold, no cherry-picker, and the elevation photographed as it is worked. In readiness for Adelaide.

In readiness

A safer way to reach what others can't.

High-access façade cleaning normally means scaffold, EWP hire and the risk that comes with working at height. Drone delivery removes the access problem — and captures condition imagery for the building record while it works.

This is a capability Summit is standing up. It will operate under the CASA credentials the work requires — held before any service is offered — and launch to existing compliance clients first. When it is live, high-access work becomes a planned service rather than a project.

01  What's included

Reach, worked
and recorded.

Façade & high-access cleaning

Glass, cladding and rendered elevations brought back to condition at height — the surfaces access equipment struggles to reach, worked without it.

Awnings, soffits & upper surfaces

The elevations that would otherwise need scaffold or a cherry-picker. Ground-level surfaces sit with pressure cleaning, available now.

Condition imagery captured

High-resolution imagery of the elevation gathered as the drone works — fed straight into the asset record for the building.

No scaffold, no EWP

No scaffold, no elevated work platform, less disruption on site — and the working-at-height exposure taken off the ground.

02  How it runs

Assess to report,
on one plan.

STAGE 01

Assess

Site and airspace review scopes the elevation, access and any constraints.

STAGE 02

Plan

SWMS, exclusion zones and a weather window set before any flight.

STAGE 03

Deliver

Drone-delivered work across the elevation, condition imagery captured as it goes.

STAGE 04

Report

Condition imagery handed into your asset record — documented, not just done.
