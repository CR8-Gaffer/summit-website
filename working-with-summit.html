Compliance posture

Stated plainly, held to.

01Director-led. The director who surveys the site owns the program, the method and the record.
02Documented method. Every service runs to a written procedure and closes with a certificate.
03No work subcontracted without your written approval.
04A site-specific safe work method statement issued before every job.
05Crews sized to the site and the window — up to six on a large program. After-hours and overnight windows offered.
06AS 1851-2012 is the only edition cited on any Summit document; acceptance criteria attributed to the AIRAH Best Practice Guide.
07What was not reached is written on the certificate before anyone asks, with the reason and the remedy.
08Based out of Adelaide, with Australia-wide coverage available.

What onboarding looks like

Six stages from request to the first certificate.

Request

STAGE 02

The pack

Returned within one business day, with any site-specific items flagged for follow-up.

STAGE 03

Platform and inductions
