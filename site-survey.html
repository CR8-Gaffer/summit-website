01  What happens

Seven steps.
Fifteen to thirty minutes.

The survey walks the exhaust system once, in a fixed order, capturing measurements and photographs as it goes. Each completed survey carries a sequential reference, SAM-SVY-####, and produces the written report.

02  The instruments

No instrument,
no survey.

An unmeasured walk-through is somebody else's product. The survey uses the certificate's kit, so the first document you receive from Summit is a working sample of the one you would be buying.

Grease-depth comb

A micron-scale wet-film reading on the duct's internal surface at every access point opened — taken at the heaviest visible accumulation within reach.

Borescope

Imagery into the duct run in both directions at each access point, labelled with the position.

Tripod light

Interior duct and plenum photography is lit so it can be assessed. A dark frame proves nothing.

Camera

Every position marked and framed so a later after-shot can be taken from the identical position.

03  The deficiency schedule

Numbered, factual,
independently quotable.

Every observation that falls short of criteria or good installation practice becomes a numbered row — a discrete scope item. The schedule itself carries no prices; it carries the location, the severity, the observation, the photo reference and the recommended action.

01Critical — fire-safety-relevant now.
02Major — will breach criteria before a next service on any reasonable interval.
03Minor — record and monitor.

Observations are measurements and facts. A reading in microns against the benchmark is an observation; an adjective is not.

04  Who gets it free

Free inside the profile. A paid condition report outside it.

The survey is free for the venues Summit is built for — hotel groups, aged care, centre food courts, venue operators and facilities-managed sites in metro Adelaide, from a single venue up.

Outside that profile, the same survey is available as a standalone condition-report engagement: same protocol, same instruments, same report. The free survey is a targeted first step, not a giveaway — evidence priced at nothing would undercut the standard the certificate exists to hold.

05  What it never does

The asset speaks.
The report writes it down.

Name, grade or characterise any provider

The report measures the system and records what it finds. If you ask whether your current arrangement is working, the answer is the deficiency schedule — you draw the conclusion.

Manufacture a gap

A system found within criteria is reported within criteria. A padded report is discoverable, and fatal to a compliance brand.

Recommend servicing where a confined-space constraint is found

Summit holds no confined-space entry competency. The constraint is recorded and nothing is recommended that cannot be delivered safely.

Assert the standard's servicing frequency

Your interval is a professional recommendation from measured accumulation, written on the report — never presented as the standard's mandated frequency.
