01What happens

Seven steps, in a fixed order.

The survey walks the exhaust system once, capturing measurements and photographs as it goes. A single kitchen typically takes 15–30 minutes; a multi-system property or a portfolio is scoped from a site list first. Each survey carries a sequential reference, SAM-SVY-####, and produces the written report.

01
Canopy and filters

Condition, loading and photographs.

02
Access points opened

A grease-depth reading at each one, position-labelled.

03
Borescope

Imagery into the duct run in both directions at each access point.

04
Fan and discharge

Condition and photographs, rooftop surrounds noted.

05
Access constraints

Recorded, with a confined-space screen. Nothing is recommended that cannot be delivered safely.

06
Compliance evidence

Existing records sighted, or their absence recorded.

07
Deficiency schedule

Every observation short of criteria becomes a numbered row: location, severity, photo reference, recommended action.

02The instruments

No instrument, no survey.

The survey uses the same instruments as the certificate, so the first document you receive from Summit is a working sample of the one you would be buying.

Grease-depth comb

Readings in microns

A wet-film reading on the duct’s internal surface at every access point opened, taken at the heaviest visible accumulation within reach.

Borescope

Both directions

Imagery into the duct run in both directions at each access point, labelled with the position.

Tripod light

Lit to be assessed

Interior duct and plenum photography is lit so it can be read. A dark frame proves nothing.

Camera

Marked positions

Every position marked and framed so a later after-shot can be taken from the identical position.

03Who gets it free

Free in metro Adelaide. A paid condition report elsewhere.

The measured survey is free for any commercial kitchen in metro Adelaide — a single restaurant, a club, a care home or a group — and you keep the written report whether or not you engage Summit.

Outside metro Adelaide the same survey is available as a paid condition report: same protocol, same instruments, same report. Any paid scope is agreed before a visit is booked.

04What it never does

The asset speaks. The report writes it down.

01
Name, grade or characterise any provider

The report measures the system and records what it finds. You draw the conclusion.

02
Manufacture a gap

A system found within criteria is reported within criteria.

03
Recommend servicing where a confined-space constraint is found

Summit holds no confined-space entry competency. The constraint is recorded and nothing is recommended that cannot be delivered safely.

04
Assert the standard’s servicing frequency

Your interval is a professional recommendation from measured accumulation, written on the report — never presented as the standard’s mandated frequency.

05After the survey

Report, proposal, program.

STAGE 01

Report delivered

Findings, the deficiency schedule and a compliance-evidence statement, in writing, kept by the venue.

STAGE 02

Proposal built

Each schedule row maps to a scope item; the interval is proposed from what was measured.

STAGE 03

Program begins

Interval set, first service booked, the year’s schedule issued. Where the survey was free, the report says so: provided without charge and without obligation.
