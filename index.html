The shift

Kitchen exhaust certificates are commonly self-declared. Summit's reports the measurements.

A sticker on the canopy and a sentence on an invoice say a visit happened. They say nothing about the duct run the sticker never saw, nothing an assessor can verify after a fire, and nothing about what was left outstanding.

Summit maintains commercial kitchen exhaust systems under AS 1851-2012 and certifies every visit with the readings, the photographs and the register on the page — evidence your insurer, your Form 3 signatory and your board can read without calling anyone.

  • Readings in microns, not adjectives
  • Photographs from identical positions
  • Next service set before the technician leaves
01  The certificate

Named to the standard,
measured to the benchmark.

Every Summit Certificate carries the same nine elements, without exception. Five of them are below; the full specimen shows all nine. The certificate summarises; behind it sits the works report and the numbered deficiency register its status is derived from. A certificate that cannot show its readings is not issued.

01Grease-depth readings in microns at every access point, before and after works.
02Before-and-after photographs from identical positions — one label per pair.
03Full-vs-partial disclosure: every section not reached, listed with the reason.
04A numbered deficiency register — location, severity, photo reference, recommended action.
05Exactly two statuses: Compliant, or Compliant with limitations — listed on the certificate itself.

04  The site survey

Start with the survey.
Keep the report either way.

A 15–30 minute measured walk of your exhaust system with the same instruments the certificate uses — grease-depth comb, borescope, tripod light, camera. You receive a written condition report and a numbered deficiency schedule whether or not you engage Summit.

01Free for hospitality groups, aged care and managed venues in metro Adelaide.

South Australia

Own a commercial building in SA? The kitchen exhaust line on your Form 3 needs evidence behind it.

Building owners lodge the Essential Safety Provisions maintenance verification within 60 business days of the calendar year's end. A signature on the strength of an unmeasured exhaust system is one the owner cannot stand behind.
